Search, centre stage — wireframes v3

No sheet and no search bar. Search takes over the whole screen. What you type is set large in the middle, the way a question looks in Ask. Dictionary matches appear under it, and Ask AI stays one tap away next to the mic. Anything that needs a closer look, like a word’s entry, opens in your existing pushback BottomSheet.

1 · Typing

The deck fades out and the text field fills the screen. There’s no field outline; the text itself is the field.

2 · Results

AI answers stay on the same screen. A word you pick opens in the pushback sheet over it, so the answer is right there when you close the sheet.

Still open

  • When Ask AI turns ink: I’d use a simple rule: two or more words, or a single word with no dictionary match. The grey Ask AI pill always works regardless.
  • Return key: it follows the ink action: search for a word, ask for a phrase. Does that feel predictable, or should return always search?
  • Follow-ups: keep the whole thread on the screen, or have each follow-up replace the answer, as Ask does today?
