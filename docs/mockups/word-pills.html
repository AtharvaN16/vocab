Word pills in AI answers

When an answer in Ask or Search mentions a word that isn't in your deck, it's shown as a raised, coloured button with a +. Tap it and the card is generated in place, the way Bencho’s Generate works: dark blurred shapes first, then edges, then fine detail, with a percentage counting up in the corner. The pill stays held down while it works, then flattens with a tick.

Try it

Tap any pill. Words already in your deck are shown as lowered pills with a tick (succinct on the right).

States

One pill, four states. Each new word gets its own colour from a fixed set, so neighbouring pills never share one.

Example sentences use a neutral raised pill instead: white in light mode, grey in dark, so it never competes with the coloured word pills.

Notes for the SwiftUI build

  • Pill: a Capsule with a solid colour fill (lighter at the top), white text, a 1 pt highlight inset at the top, a darker bottom edge and a tight, neutral drop shadow, like a key. No coloured glow. When pressed it scales to 0.95 and moves down 1 pt, matching the existing PressStyle.
  • Inline in text: SwiftUI Text can't hold views. Either lay out the tokens with a flow Layout (each word is a view, so you get real buttons), or render the answer as AttributedString and draw the pills over the measured runs. A flow layout is simpler and works with VoiceOver.
  • Forming card (after Bencho’s Generate): over the whole run t, three overlapping windows. Shapes: blur 20 pt and contrast 2.4 (pivoting on the card’s paper colour, so the ground holds and the ink deepens). Form (t 0.1→0.82): blur falls to 1.6 on a 1−(1−form)^2.2 curve, contrast eases to 1, and a noise ripple of 32·ripple·(1−t)^1.5 stills. Grain (t 0.7→1): the sharp card fades in on top. The % climbs on an uneven, re-rolled curve, caps at 99 until the card lands, then holds 100 for 0.65 s. In SwiftUI: TimelineView driving .blur, .contrast and a Metal .distortionEffect; the filter only exists during the run.
  • Add pill: same shape and press behaviour, as a neutral key (white / grey). It appends the sentence to the word’s examples (shown on WordDetailView), and a word pill inside the same sentence still works on its own.
  • Reduce Motion: no blur or ripple; the card fades in already sharp over 0.4 s and the count still runs.
  • Haptics: light impact on press, success notification when the tick appears.
