What each one actually changes

Each option combines two separate choices. 1. Does the page shrink? If it does, something has to show around it, and that's the rim. It's black today because BottomSheet puts Color.black behind the page. Black isn't required; switch the rim above to compare. 2. What covers the page? A tint (black or page colour) and/or a blur.
